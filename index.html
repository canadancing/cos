"Homemade food with love is the only way we cook."

Founded by Chef Valma, Caribbean of Soul has been nourishing North Vancouver since 2013. We believe soul food isn't just a recipe—it is patience, tradition, and heart.

From hand-marinating our jerk chicken with traditional pimento berries to slow-simmering our oxtail so the gravy turns rich and gelatinous, every plate is prepared fresh. Whether you pick up hot dinner after work or stock your home freezer with our dozen patty packs, we treat you like family.
